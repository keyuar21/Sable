How to read this. A DR reduces a customer or merchant account; a CR increases it. A UPI payment is two separate entries on two separate banks: the remitter posts DR customer / CR POOL, and the beneficiary posts DR POOL / CR merchant. No entry ever spans two banks — that is why the two pool balances must cancel, and why a failed credit leg leaves the money recoverable in the remitter's pool instead of destroying it.
